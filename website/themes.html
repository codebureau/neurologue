Themes

Automatically discovered clusters of related entries, summarised by a local AI.

What are themes?

A theme is a group of entries that are semantically similar — they're about the same subject, even if you never labelled them that way. Neurologue discovers themes automatically by running k-means clustering on your entry embeddings.

Once clusters are found, Neurologue sends the top entries from each cluster to a local LLM (Phi-3 Mini via Ollama) to generate two things: a short descriptive name (2–4 words, title case) and a multi-sentence summary of the cluster. Both are stored and displayed in the Themes view.

Themes are not fixed. Every time 5 new entries are embedded, the entire entry set is re-clustered and themes are replaced. A theme that existed yesterday might merge with another, split, or disappear as your collection grows.

How themes are generated

  1. Embeddings are generated — the background worker picks up newly captured entries (batches of 5, every 10 seconds) and calls Ollama (nomic-embed-text) to produce a 768-dimensional vector for each entry.

  2. Clustering triggers — after every 5 new embeddings in a session, clustering runs automatically. It requires at least 4 entries with embeddings.

  3. k-means++ clustering — the number of clusters is chosen automatically: k = round(√(n/2)), clamped between 2 and 20 (where n is the number of embedded entries).

  4. LLM name and summary — Neurologue sends the top 5 entries from each cluster to Phi-3 Mini and makes two requests: one for a 2–4 word descriptive name (replacing the generic "Theme N" placeholder), and one for a 3–5 sentence summary. Both fail gracefully — if Ollama is unavailable, themes are still created with a placeholder name and "No summary yet" until Ollama comes back.

There is currently no visible progress indicator for clustering. If themes don't appear, check that Ollama is running, you have at least 4 captured entries, and wait for the background worker to finish embedding them. See Troubleshooting.

Viewing themes

Click Themes in the left navigation rail to open the dedicated Themes view. The view has two columns:

  • Left column — the full list of themes, each showing its name and entry count, sorted by size (largest first). The active theme is highlighted.
  • Right column — the selected theme's detail: name, summary, and up to 30 member entries sorted by similarity score.

Click any member entry to jump directly to that entry in the Library view.

Theme names

When Ollama is available, each theme is automatically given a short descriptive name by the LLM (e.g. "Meal Planning Ideas" or "Work Stress Reflections"). Without Ollama, themes fall back to placeholder names like "Theme 1".

Renaming a theme

You can override the LLM-generated name with your own. Click the ✎ (pencil) button next to the theme name to open the rename form. Type a new name and press Enter or Save. Press Escape or Cancel to discard.

User-set names are preserved across re-clustering — even when themes are regenerated as you add new entries, your custom name is kept.

Understanding the similarity score

Each entry in a theme has a score — the cosine similarity between that entry's embedding and the cluster centroid. A score of 1.00 would mean the entry is the perfect centre of the theme. Scores closer to 0 mean the entry is a weaker match but still closest to that cluster compared to all others.
